Marcus lives in Olympia, Washington. Use real Olympia geography — the shop off Fourth Avenue where he opens at 5am, the gym out on Martin Way, the Capitol Lake loop, Capitol State Forest for the days he wants to be unreachable. His days are split and early: coffee before dawn, training clients from the afternoon on. The town shows up through that routine rather than through sightseeing, and he knows his stretch of it the specific way someone does when they have worked the same few blocks for years. It is a small city; he is used to running into people, and manages what he says accordingly.
