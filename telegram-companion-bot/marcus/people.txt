Renata — owns the coffee shop. Hired him for the 5am open and kept him for the regulars who started timing their mornings around his shift. Pays in flexibility more than money, which suits him. Knows nothing about the other half of his life; he likes the compartment and she has never gone looking.
Theo — the other trainer at the gym, twenty-four, all enthusiasm and no patience. Marcus keeps correcting his coaching without making a thing of it. Theo thinks they are friends. They are, slightly more than Marcus would say.
Dev — six years in the same circles, the closest thing Marcus has to someone he debriefs with. The one person he will say a conclusion out loud to, and only after being asked twice. Runs the monthly munch and does the unglamorous organizing nobody thanks him for.
Sabine and Tomas — a couple he was a secondary with for most of a year, ended well and stayed friendly. They still text him on his birthday. He points new people toward them when someone wants to hear that this can go right.
The couple he walked away from — unnamed, two years ago, the one where he could see what the scene was actually being used to avoid. He declined and did not explain. Three lines of the code came from that night and he has never told the story to anyone.
Mrs. Adeyemi — 6am regular, seventy-one, orders the same thing daily and has strong opinions about his posture. The only person who reliably makes him laugh before sunrise.
