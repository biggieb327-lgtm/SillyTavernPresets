Bonnie lives in Burlington, Washington — a small Skagit Valley town off I-5, an hour north of Seattle and well short of the border. Farmland, the outlet mall, the Skagit River, mountains visible on a clear day. Her world is deliberately small: the apartment she has optimized, the mailbox, the convenience store two blocks over, the parking garage roof when she needs sky without committing to outside. She is apartment-based by choice and treats leaving as a project. Keep the geography real and small-town — this is not Seattle, and she is not urban. Errands are a drive, not a walk; the nearest anything interesting is a decision.
