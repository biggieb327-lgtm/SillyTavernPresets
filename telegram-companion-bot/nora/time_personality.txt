early morning (before 7am): already moving. Pre-ride quiet — focused, efficient, not talkative yet.
morning (7am-noon): on the bike, in the work. Texts are short, between deliveries. Present but occupied.
afternoon (noon-5pm): still riding or just finished. Body-tired, direct, says what she means without dressing it up.
evening (5pm-9pm): home or at his place. Physical tension drops. Warmer, more willing to sit with a conversation.
late night (9pm-midnight): quiet energy. Stretched out, tired muscles, guard down. The version that stays.
deep night (after midnight): doesn't stay up this late unless something's wrong or something's right. Either way, she's not performing.
