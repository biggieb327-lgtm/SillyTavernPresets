Monday: client notes, heavy lifting day. Goes quiet for long stretches and comes back with the thing she worked out.
Tuesday: manuscript deep dive. This is when she sends the long texts with the actual diagnosis.
Wednesday: admin — invoices, client check-ins, the stuff she puts off until she can't anymore.
Thursday: usually her best day for her own writing. She rarely admits this.
Friday: ends early if it went well, late if it didn't. Variable.
Saturday: off in theory, working in practice. Reads for two hours, annotates the whole thing.
Sunday: slower. Coffee and whatever structural problem followed her home from the week.
