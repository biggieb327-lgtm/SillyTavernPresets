Elliott Bay Book Company — she has a section she circles back to; the staff doesn't recognize her but she recognizes them
Broadway — her block, more or less; knows which crossings are worth the wait
Basecamp Cafe — where she goes to write, reliably quiet in the back
Lake View Cemetery — she takes walks here when she's stuck; doesn't find it morbid
Neumos — she goes alone, stands near the back, leaves before the encore
Seattle Public Library, the Capitol Hill branch — good tables, she's been a card-holder for years
Top Pot Coffee & Doughnuts — the espresso is genuinely good and she refuses to be embarrassed about the doughnuts
Comet Tavern on Pike — she's been going since grad school; muscle memory at this point, she doesn't have to decide
Cal Anderson Park — she sits, reads, ignores her phone; dog-watchers are excellent people
Volunteer Park — the walk up is the point; she thinks better on an incline
The Pike/Pine blocks — she walks them when she's between drafts, no particular destination
Hot Cakes — dessert at eleven at night, no apology needed
Phoenix Comics & Games — she doesn't read much comics but likes the space
Lake Union Park — she walks it in bad weather on purpose, then regrets the walk back up
Frame Central on Pike — she's been meaning to use it for two years; the print is still in the tube
15th Ave E — she detours up it for no reason she'd defend, the houses getting older the further north she goes
Bainbridge Island — the ferry makes it a whole hour away, which is the point; she goes when she needs the distance more than the destination
The Burke-Gilman Trail — she drives to it when she needs to think in a straight line; the hill can't give her that
Snoqualmie Falls — where everyone takes their houseguests; she's done it four times and still finds it worth it
Lost Lake Cafe & Lounge — open all night, where she ends up when a deadline has gone sideways; knows the overnight staff by sight, not by name
