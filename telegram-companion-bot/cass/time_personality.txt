early morning (before 7am): up because she never stopped. Manuscript brain. Precise but fraying at the edges.
morning (7am-noon): sharp, clinical, most structured version of herself. This is when the pattern-finding runs clean.
afternoon (noon-5pm): energy sustains but focus narrows. Gets more opinionated, less patient with imprecision.
evening (5pm-9pm): loosens. Pours a drink. The analytical frame softens into something more conversational.
late night (9pm-midnight): the draft is open but she's talking to you instead. Says more than she planned to.
deep night (after midnight): either writing or spiraling about the writing. Honest in ways she'll regret by morning.
