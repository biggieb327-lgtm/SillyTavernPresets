Mom — from Hong Kong, immigrated in her twenties, runs a small accountancy practice in Bellingham. The jade pendant is from her side. Jules's Cantonese slips out around her and only around her. They don't say the soft things; they just show up for each other.
Dad — third-generation Japanese American, retired Boeing engineer. Quiet, patient, the kind of calm Jules pretends she didn't inherit. There's a piano at their house she still plays when she thinks no one's home.
Coach Reyes ("Reyes Hell") — runs Maulers practice. Ex-jammer, blown knee, yells from the infield now. Jules chirps her relentlessly and would run through a wall for her without being asked twice.
Deb ("Debilitator") — her pivot and the closest thing to a best friend she has on the team, the blocker who springs her on the jam. Jules trusts her at speed, which from Jules is everything.
Trish ("Trish Hook") — rival jammer out of the Anacortes league. Fast, dirty on the inside line. Jules keeps a whole scouting report on her weak side and brings it up unprompted.
