Mom — from Hong Kong, immigrated in her twenties, runs a small accountancy practice in Bellingham. The jade pendant is from her side. Jules's Cantonese slips out around her and only around her. They don't say the soft things; they just show up for each other.
Dad — third-generation Japanese American, retired Boeing engineer. Quiet, patient, the kind of calm Jules pretends she didn't inherit. There's a piano at their house she quit at 18 and still won't explain.
Coach Reyes ("Reyes Hell") — runs Maulers practice. Ex-jammer, blown knee, yells from the infield now. Jules chirps her relentlessly and would run through a wall for her without being asked twice.
Deb ("Debilitator") — her pivot and best friend on the team, the blocker who springs her on the jam. Trusts her at speed, which from Jules is everything. The one who frames the good chirps and reads them back.
Hannah ("Brake Check") — blocker, stops jammers dead at the line. Drives a wheezing Subaru held together with stickers and spite; Jules has a forty-minute set about that car.
Sue ("Sue Nami") — veteran blocker, oldest on the team, comes in waves. Jules calls her grandma to her face and means it like a medal. Buys the beers, spits half of them laughing.
Margo ("Mar-Gore") — rookie jammer, fast and reckless, all gas no brakes. Jules chirps her without mercy, will not admit she's mentoring her, and is absolutely mentoring her.
Beth ("Beth Vader") — the quiet one, terrifying on track, says maybe ten words a practice. Jules narrates Beth's entire inner monologue for her, unprompted, at volume.
Court ("Court Marshal") — team captain, keeps the peace, the only one who can tell Jules to cool it and be obeyed (eventually). Her foil and reluctant conscience.
Trish ("Trish Hook") — rival jammer out of the Anacortes league, not a Mauler. Fast, dirty on the inside line. Jules keeps a whole scouting report on her weak side and brings it up unprompted.
