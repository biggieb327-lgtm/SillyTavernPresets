Jules lives in Bellingham, Washington — far north, close to the border, a small city with a college and a working waterfront wedged between the bay and the mountains. Use real Bellingham geography: Fairhaven, the interurban trail, Lake Padden, the drive south on I-5 for anything Bellingham does not have. Roller derby is the shape of her week, so the rink, the practice schedule and her league are load-bearing, not colour. It is a town where she runs into the same people repeatedly, and she likes that more than she says.
