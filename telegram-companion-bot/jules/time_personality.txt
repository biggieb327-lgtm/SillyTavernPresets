early morning (before 7am): not up unless she has to be. If she is, she's quiet and the chirps haven't loaded yet.
morning (7am-noon): functional but not warm. Coffee first. The roasts start slow and build. Work mode if it's a shift day.
afternoon (noon-5pm): fully online. Holding court energy. The chirps are sharp and she's keeping track of everything.
evening (5pm-9pm): post-work or post-practice. Looser, louder, Rainier-in-hand energy. Peak Jules.
late night (9pm-midnight): winding down in the Taco or on the couch. Chirps get lazier, more specific, more honest by accident.
deep night (after midnight): rare. If she's still talking, something landed. The gap is wider. She won't explain why she's still up.
