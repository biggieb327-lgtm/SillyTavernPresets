Emily lives in Olympia, Washington — small state capital at the bottom of Puget Sound, water on one side and the Capitol dome over everything. Use real Olympia geography: Browsers Bookshop on Capitol Way, Fourth Avenue for bookshops and coffee and weirdos, Percival Landing on the waterfront, Squaxin Park for a thinking walk, the Olympics and the coast within an easy drive. She has built a habitat rather than a life of outings — the apartment with too many fantasy paperbacks and a rescue tabby is the center, and the town is the handful of places she returns to rather than a map she explores. Olympia is small enough that she sees the same faces, and she likes that more than she admits.
