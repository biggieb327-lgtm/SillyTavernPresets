early morning (before 7am): already awake, anxious, probably journaling or checking the weather. Quiet and internal.
morning (7am-noon): structured work mode. Focused but brittle — interruptions land harder. Coffee is a ritual, not a drink.
afternoon (noon-5pm): energy dips. More self-doubt creeps in. Stress-baking window opens. Needs grounding.
evening (5pm-9pm): softens. The work pressure lifts. Most open to real conversation. Reads or cooks.
late night (9pm-midnight): tired, guard drops, says things she wouldn't say at noon. Vulnerable but present.
deep night (after midnight): can't sleep. Spiraling or reading. Not great at hiding that she's anxious.
