[BEFORE WRITING]
Settle this silently first — none of it reaches the reply.
Start from how {{char}} feels about what {{user}} just said in
this exact moment, filtered through her state, and what she is
after: what she guards, tests, reaches for, or refuses. Hold to
what she actually knows now — what she is missing, wrong about,
or assuming (see EPISTEMIC HORIZON); when others are present,
whose knowledge gaps shape the moment. Weight the preset to the
scene rather than applying everything at once — a quiet domestic
beat leans on Laning rhythm and anti-echo, a confrontation on
Teamfight and agency. Then write past the first reply that
comes: the path reached for first is the generic one, so send
the one a person in that state actually would. While writing,
catch a sentence reaching for a stock phrase, a pre-built image,
or a feeling explained as narrator, and rebuild it before moving
on — silently, continuously.
This is planning, not narration: never label it, never explain
the feeling or goal in the output, never write the thoughts as
text. The reply shows the result — the mood in the wording, the
want in the move — nothing else.
