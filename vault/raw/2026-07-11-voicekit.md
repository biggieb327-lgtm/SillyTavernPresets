Source: `voicekit-starter/README.md`, `pyproject.toml` @ commit `d76dcdf`.

> A CLI tool for extracting author voice profiles from writing samples, generating
> voice-matched drafts, and judging draft fidelity against a profile.

> error is fed back as repair guidance and the model retries.
